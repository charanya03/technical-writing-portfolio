# Quick-Start Guide: Creating a New Course Module in WordPress

## 📋 Context
* **Goal:** Guide non-technical instructors through creating and publishing a new structured learning module.
* **Target Audience:** Course Creators, Teachers, and Site Administrators.
* **Tools Used:** Markdown, WordPress LMS (LearnDash/LifterLMS), Elementor Page Builder.

---

### 📌 Overview
This guide helps you create a new course module, add lessons to it, and restrict access to registered students. Follow these steps to ensure your content layouts render correctly on both desktop and mobile devices.

### ⏱️ Prerequisites
Before you begin, ensure you have:
* An active **Instructor** or **Administrator** account on the WordPress dashboard.
* Your course syllabus and text lessons drafted.

---

### 🚀 Step-by-Step Instructions

#### Step 1: Access the Course Builder
1. Log into your WordPress Dashboard (`://yourdomain.com`).
2. In the left-hand sidebar, navigate to **LMS Courses** > **Courses**.
3. Hover over the title of the course you want to edit and click **Edit**.
4. At the top of the screen, click the **Builder** tab to open the visual curriculum planner.

#### Step 2: Create a New Module Section
1. Scroll to the bottom of the Builder interface and click **+ New Section**.
2. Type a clear, descriptive title for your module (e.g., *Module 1: Introduction to Web Design*).
3. Press **Enter** or click **Add Section**. Your new module placeholder will appear at the bottom of the curriculum list.

#### Step 3: Attach Lessons to the Module
1. Look at the right-hand sidebar under the **Lessons** pool.
2. Click and hold the drag icon (⠿) next to your pre-written lesson.
3. Drag the lesson over to your newly created module and drop it directly underneath the module title.
4. Repeat this process for all lessons belonging to this specific module.

#### Step 4: Configure Access Settings
1. Click the small **Gear Icon** (⚙️) on the right side of your module bar to open the settings panel.
2. Under **Access Mode**, select one of the following options:
   * **Public:** Anyone can view the content without logging in.
   * **Buy Now:** Locked until the student purchases the course.
   * **Drip-Feed:** Automatically unlocks X days after the student enrolls.
3. Click **Apply Changes**.

---

### 💡 Visual Layout Pro-Tips
* **Keep Titles Short:** Keep lesson titles under 60 characters so they do not wrap awkwardly on mobile screens.
* **Save Progress:** The builder auto-saves your layout every 2 minutes, but it is best practice to click the blue **Update** button in the top right corner before exiting the page.

---

### 🔍 Troubleshooting Common Issues

| Problem | Cause | Solution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Lessons appear out of order. | The drag-and-drop hierarchy was not saved. | Go back to the **Builder** tab, re-order the items, and click **Update**. |
| Students see a "404 Error" page. | The course or lesson is still saved as a draft. | Open the specific lesson page and change its status from **Draft** to **Published**. |
| Video blocks do not load. | Incorrect video URL format used in the editor. | Ensure you are using the direct embed link from Vimeo or YouTube, not the standard watch link. |
